# Changelog

All notable changes to TuneLib are documented in this file.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project
adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Every released version needs a `## [X.Y.Z] - YYYY-MM-DD` section here: the release workflow
reads it with `scripts/extract-changelog.cjs` and refuses to publish a tag without one.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-21

### Added

- Persistent library of manually added tracks, with several libraries side by side and a
  switcher next to the library name.
- Adding files through the system dialog or by dropping them on the window, and removing
  an entry without deleting the file on disk.
- Metadata editing for title, artist, album, year, genre and cover art, written back into
  the tags of MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG and WAV files. Bulk editing across a selection.
- List view with sortable, resizable, reorderable and hideable columns, and preview view
  with cover art on a grid; the chosen view is remembered.
- Artists, Albums and Genres sections, each opening a detail panel for the group.
- Text search, missing-information filter and reporting of files that moved or disappeared.
- Player docked at the bottom of the window and expandable to full page, with seeking,
  volume, shuffle, repeat-one, and the visible list as its queue.
- Settings for language (Italian and English), text size, light/dark/system theme, accent
  colour, ambient background and glass surfaces.
- In-app guide, custom window titlebar and cover-derived colour for the player.
